# Centerprise public website inventory

Reviewed on 22 November 2026 for the [business and competitor analysis](company-and-market.md). The starting point was the [corporate home page](https://www.centerprise.co.uk/). Its main menu repeats on the page. After removing repeated menu entries, 98 destinations pointed to the corporate domain, 12 to CiLifecycle and one to an external customer form.

The table covers each of the 98 corporate destinations. **Page** means material was retrieved from the destination. **Preview** means a public preview resolving to the same canonical URL supplied the text after the menu route timed out. **PDF** means the public document was opened and its purpose checked. The totals were 29 pages, 66 previews and three PDFs. The topic column records what each destination contributed to the review. The linked business analysis discusses the services and cases most relevant to the role.
